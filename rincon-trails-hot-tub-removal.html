FAQ: Hot Tub Removal in Rincon Trails

Do I need to disconnect the power first?

Yes. For safety and liability reasons, the electrical power must be disconnected by a licensed electrician before we can begin demolition and hauling.

Do I need to drain the hot tub?

Yes. The unit should be fully drained and dry before our crew arrives to prevent water from spilling during the demolition and transport.

Can you remove a tub built into a deck?

Yes. We can handle spa demolition for units that are recessed or built into decks. Please provide photos so we can accurately quote the additional labor.

What do you do with the old hot tub?

We dismantle the unit and sort the materials. Components like pumps and heaters are recycled, while the shell is disposed of at a licensed facility.
