FAQ: Garage Cleanouts in Coyote Creek

Do I need to box everything up first?

No! We can haul items loose or in boxes. If you've already started sorting, that's great, but it's not required.

What happens to the junk you take?

We prioritize donation for usable items and recycling for scrap metal. Anything left over is disposed of responsibly at local facilities.

Do you take hazardous materials from the garage?

We cannot haul wet paint, chemicals, oils, or pressurized tanks. Please contact Pima County waste for hazardous material disposal.

How long does a garage cleanout take?

Most standard two-car garage cleanouts are completed in 1-3 hours depending on the volume of junk.
