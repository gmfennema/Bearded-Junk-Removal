Can you remove a piano from a second floor?

Yes, depending on the layout of the stairs. Pianos are extremely heavy, so safety is our first priority. Please provide photos of the stairs for an accurate assessment.

What do you do with the old piano?

We prioritize donation for pianos that are still in working order. If the piano is broken or beyond repair, we take it to a licensed disposal facility where the metal components can be recycled.
