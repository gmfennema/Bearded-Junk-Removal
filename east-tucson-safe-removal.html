FAQ: Heavy Safe Removal in East Tucson

Can you move a safe up or down stairs?

This depends on the weight and the structure of the stairs. Please send photos and details about the path of removal so we can assess it safely.

Do you need the combination?

We only need the safe to be empty and the door to be closed/locked for safe transport. We do not need the combination unless the safe is bolted to the floor from the inside.

What do you do with old safes?

We prioritize recycling for the heavy metal components. If the safe is still functional and in good condition, we may explore donation or resale options.
