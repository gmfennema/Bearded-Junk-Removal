FAQ: Shed Removal in Rocking K Ranch

Do I need to empty the shed first?

It’s helpful, but not required. We can provide a combined quote for clearing out the contents and then demolishing the shed structure.

Can you remove a shed on a concrete slab?

Yes. We dismantle the shed down to the slab. If you also need the concrete slab removed, please let us know as that requires specialized heavy hauling.

What if the shed has electricity?

For safety and liability reasons, any electrical power must be disconnected by a licensed professional before we can begin demolition.
